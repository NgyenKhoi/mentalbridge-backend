# Identity account deletion and data retention policy v0.3

- **Engineering Policy Decision:** PROPOSED. **Formal Approval:** PENDING PRIVACY/SECURITY, LEGAL AND TECHNICAL REVIEW. This document is not an accepted policy, a legal determination, or authorization to delete production data.
- **Owner and scope:** Identity-owned account, authentication and recovery data. Identity does not delete data owned by other services. Production intake and cross-service completion are outside this proposal and remain dependent on MB-777.
- **Sources:** [MB-778 proposal v0.2, comment 10221](https://vunguyenkhoi47.atlassian.net/browse/MB-778?focusedCommentId=10221), [CHANGES REQUIRED, comment 10252](https://vunguyenkhoi47.atlassian.net/browse/MB-778?focusedCommentId=10252), and [Identity account and session policy](../modules/identity-security-policy.md). This version refines the Jira proposal without representing it as approved.

## Current baseline and gap

At the current `dev` baseline, [`AccountEntity.markDeleted()`](../../identity-service/src/main/java/com/mentalbridge/identity/account/AccountEntity.java) changes status and timestamps; it does not erase email or password hash. The [Identity baseline migration](../../identity-service/src/main/resources/db/changelog/changes/001-identity-baseline.sql) requires both `account.email` and `password_hash`; email is `citext` with a unique constraint. Registration and login normalize email before lookup. The baseline also stores one-time-token hashes and refresh-session token hashes. An inventory must verify additional copies, lookup keys, idempotency payloads, delivery queues and provider-held data; this document does not assert that the inventory is complete.

The coordinator checks its recorded hold before retrying Identity deletion, marks the account DELETED and revokes refresh sessions. Its [current deletion model](../../identity-service/src/main/resources/db/changelog/changes/006-retention-and-deletion-governance.sql) records one hold type per request; it is not proof that independently scoped or concurrent holds are enforced across all owners. [MB-779](https://github.com/NgyenKhoi/mentalbridge-backend/pull/233) is merged into `dev`: Identity checks account status locally, and Care, Community and Consultation use the Identity authorization boundary. Deployment, all other consumers, availability and the release gate still require verification. Neither DELETED nor MB-779 establishes that the data-retention policy is approved or that field erasure has occurred.

## Proposed account and field treatment

1. When an authorized, verified deletion takes effect, the account remains terminal `DELETED`: immediately deny login, password recovery, refresh and account access; revoke active refresh sessions and reject previously issued access JWTs at participating protected services. A hold never re-enables authentication. Preserve fail-closed behavior if the current status cannot be verified.
2. When the field is eligible for erasure under the approved schedule and all applicable holds and lawful exceptions have been checked, erase the operational email and every retained normalized email/lookup key derived from it. Prefer removal to a reversible mask. The current `NOT NULL` and unique `citext` constraint prevents this without a separately reviewed migration and lookup design.
3. On DELETED, immediately invalidate outstanding verification/recovery challenges and refresh credentials while preserving any metadata subject to a hold. No credential may remain usable for authentication after DELETED, including when a hold requires preserving the password hash under restricted access. At the field-eligibility decision, erase the password hash; remove challenge and session identifiers, hashes and related copies when their separately approved retention and hold conditions permit. A keyed or unkeyed hash, encrypted copy, lookup index or tombstone can remain personal or linkable data; do not treat hashing as automatic anonymization.
4. If a field cannot be erased because a specific approved obligation or technical constraint applies, preserve only the covered minimum with restricted access and an expiry/review decision. Pseudonymization is a proposed fallback only after Privacy/Security and DB-owner approval: use a non-reversible, non-email-derived surrogate that cannot authenticate or match the old email. Do not use a fake address containing or deterministically derived from the original. The chosen representation, keys and any re-identification risk need approval; it is not yet an implementation decision.

## Trigger, deadlines and exceptions

The proposed trigger for erasure is **all** of: an authenticated and authorized request with a committed terminal DELETED state; an approved per-field legal/retention eligibility decision; and confirmation that no applicable hold blocks that field. The executing Identity owner must re-check these conditions immediately before an irreversible operation, record the decision and outcome atomically with its own state, and fail closed if the hold or eligibility authority is unavailable. `DELETED` alone, expiry of one timer, and release of one hold are not sufficient proof of eligibility. Define transaction/locking and recovery behavior in a separate implementation design; no distributed atomicity is assumed.

The erasure execution deadline, legal bases and exceptions are **PENDING FORMAL APPROVAL**. For a qualifying personal-data deletion request made through the proper procedure, the proposed response target is two working days, with fulfillment within 20 days, or 30 days where a processor or third party must provide, erase or restrict processing of the data. A single extension of at most 20 days is proposed only where conditions are met, the person is notified of the reason, and necessity and reasonableness are documented. Refer to [Law 91/2025/QH15](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=1&docid=214590&pageid=27160&typegroup=) and [Article 5(4) of Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP, official text](https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2026/01/356-nd.signed.pdf). Privacy/Legal must confirm applicability to MentalBridge, the start event, lawful exceptions and extension conditions before issuance; pending policy approval does not suspend applicable legal obligations. These proposed request targets do not define internal retention periods. Track overdue requests and escalate without fabricating completion or erasing held data.

## Holds and resumption

| Hold | Proposed authority and scope | Release and retry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `LEGAL_HOLD` | Only a designated, authorized Legal/Privacy authority may approve the legal basis, subject, fields, purpose, access restrictions and review/expiry condition. | The authorized authority records release; independently re-evaluate all other holds, obligations and eligibility before a new deletion attempt. |
| `OPERATIONAL_HOLD` | A designated operations/security authority may approve only a specific documented operational or safety need and minimum affected scope; it is not a blanket indefinite retention permission. | The responsible authority records resolution and release; re-evaluate all other holds and retention before retry. |
| `DISPUTE_HOLD` | A designated dispute/legal authority may approve the specific dispute, necessary fields and duration/review condition. | The authority records resolution/release; re-evaluate all other holds and retention before retry. |

The accountable approvers, exact role mapping, allowed reasons, independent hold representation and precedence are **PENDING APPROVAL**. Each hold must record its reason, affected field scope, responsible authority and review schedule. The proposed review interval is every 30 days; review does not automatically release or extend the hold. Keep any restricted hold registry separate from ordinary audit evidence, with safe reason codes and no raw case content in ordinary audit records. Preserve fields covered by any active applicable hold; an unknown scope, absent or unavailable authority blocks irreversible deletion. Release does not itself erase data or reactivate a user: a separately authorized, idempotent retry must re-check every competing hold and lawful retention condition. Concurrent hold application and erasure must serialize at the owning boundary; owner-specific holds must be enforced by that owner, not by cross-service database access. These are proposed requirements, not a claim that the current single-hold coordinator already implements them.

## Re-registration and uniqueness

**Proposed:** only after the original email and normalized lookup keys are lawfully eligible for release and release has completed, a new registration may use that email as a new account UUID after fresh ownership verification. It must not restore the deleted identity, prior sessions, recovery challenges, roles, owner data or access. Do not reveal whether a deleted/held account exists beyond the approved generic registration/recovery contract. If an applicable hold or lawful obligation prevents lawful release of the email, registration with that email is not allowed while the block remains.

**Proposed implementation direction:** make email and password-hash fields nullable through a separate implementation task and retain uniqueness for every email still in use or lawfully reserved, including held emails. The DB owner must approve the unique-index and concurrency design, lookup and idempotency behavior, and safe migration/rollback. A non-reversible surrogate is a fallback only if separately approved. The current unique `citext` value prevents the proposed re-registration while retained. Do not infer that an email can be safely released merely because `status` is DELETED. Privacy/Security must approve reuse timing, anti-enumeration behavior and any abuse-prevention exception.

## Minimum audit and backup/restore

Proposed audit evidence contains only the minimum event UUID, opaque/linkable subject or tombstone reference, actor/service, action, outcome, timestamp, correlation ID, policy version and approved safe hold/reason codes. Do not put raw email, normalized keys, password/hash, session/recovery tokens, secrets, journal, chat or health content in evidence. Restrict access, record access, minimize linkage and periodically review necessity. UUIDs and hashes can still identify or link a person and require protection.

The **proposed** audit default is 90 days after request closure, only while a documented lawful retention basis and purpose remain; erase sooner when they no longer apply. Retention beyond that default requires an approved purpose, legal basis and access controls, with a proposed cap of 12 months. A legally required exception must be recorded and approved separately with restricted scope and review/expiry. Neither 90 days nor 12 months is a statutory requirement or an approved period. Privacy/Security and Legal must approve the actual durations, legal bases, hold treatment and disposal.

Backup copies and provider exports need an approved inventory, encryption/access controls, expiry schedule and hold-aware disposal. The proposed backup lifecycle target is at most 30 days; it is not an established schedule, legal retention period or guarantee. Operations must verify the actual schedule and approve exceptions. On restore, keep restored data isolated from user traffic and downstream processing until an independently durable, access-controlled deletion/tombstone ledger, current authoritative holds and deletion eligibility have been reconciled. Reapply eligible erasures without exposing restored identifiers, preserve data still lawfully held, and validate the result before reopening access. Missing or stale authoritative ledger/hold information fails closed and requires escalation. Tech Lead, DB and operations owners must approve ledger ownership, durability, reconciliation, retention, backup expiry and tested recovery procedures; this is a proposed control, not an existing implementation claim.

## Decisions and implementation gate

| Proposed decision | Required owner | Acceptance condition; formal status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Erase eligible identity fields; block authentication immediately; permit new UUID only after lawful email release | Privacy/Security with Legal | Approve field scope, lawful exceptions, release and re-registration rules; PENDING |
| Three scoped holds with proposed 30-day review; release permits re-evaluation only | Legal/Privacy, Operations/Security and Dispute/Legal authorities | Designate authorities and approve scope, reason codes, review and fail-closed rules; PENDING |
| Minimal audit; proposed 90-day default and 12-month cap | Privacy/Security with Legal | Approve purpose, lawful basis, access, exceptions and retention; PENDING |
| Nullable email/password hash and uniqueness for used or reserved emails | Tech Lead and Identity DB owner | Approve constraints, concurrency, lookup and separate migration plan; PENDING |
| Proposed 30-day backup target and fail-closed restore reconciliation | Operations, DB and Tech Lead with Privacy/Security | Verify actual lifecycle; approve exceptions, ledger and restore validation; PENDING |
| Request timelines and protected-service coverage | Legal, Tech Lead and Security | Confirm applicability, exceptions, service authentication, capacity and release readiness; PENDING |

After accountable approval, create a separate implementation follow-up if changes are required. Its targeted tests must cover field removal and constraints; old-email re-registration and concurrency; invalidated login/recovery/refresh and existing JWTs; multiple, overlapping and unavailable holds; release/retry and transaction failure; audit minimization; and backup/restore reconciliation. Do not change `markDeleted()`, execute live erasure, infer owner completion or mark MB-778 Done based on this proposal.
