# MB-596 product journey metrics

The ADMIN-only `GET /api/v1/admin/product-journey-metrics` projection accepts an explicit half-open UTC window capped at 366 days. Identity composes USER-only registration-cohort progress with bounded aggregate responses from Care and Consultation over REST. SPECIALIST and ADMIN accounts are excluded from the end-user journey. Every source reports a version and as-of instant; a failed dependency produces `UNAVAILABLE` stages with null counts and rates rather than inferred zeroes.

The projection contains aggregate counts and explicitly scoped cohort rates only. It has no cohort breakdown, account identifier, PHQ-9/GAD-7 score or band, assessment answer, Journal or emotion content, AI analysis, consultation participant, chat, private note, or user-level outcome. Identity activation counts only USER accounts registered in `[from,to)` whose authoritative `email_verified_at` is before `to`. Consultation confirmation and completion count only appointments requested in `[from,to)` that reached the corresponding immutable lifecycle milestone before `to`. These three rate-bearing stages divide the matching numerator by its named cohort stage and round to one decimal place; a zero denominator or a stage without an approved relationship returns `rate=null`. The response explicitly labels all metrics as descriptive product activity, not clinical effectiveness or causation.

Care owns completed screening episodes, generated Support Guides, and paid SupportPlan activations. No authoritative Support Guide open fact exists, so that stage remains explicitly unavailable. Care counts remain factual totals with no invented cross-stage rate because no approved cohort relationship exists in this scope.

Focused verification covers USER-role filtering, registration and appointment cohort boundaries, lifecycle transitions at/after `to`, zero denominators, impossible owner cohort counts, endpoint-level ADMIN/USER/SPECIALIST/anonymous authorization, dependency degradation, source/contract versions, null unavailable counts/rates, and absence of sensitive response fields. The frontend consumes the same Identity OpenAPI projection through an ADMIN-authenticated same-origin BFF and verifies that the authoritative response window exactly matches the request.
