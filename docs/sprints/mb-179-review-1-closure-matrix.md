## Open administrative closure actions

These actions do not reopen the approved product decisions:

1. Mentor or supervisor records Review 1 closure validation against this matrix.
2. Jira subtask statuses and review comments are updated with links to the blueprint, policies and pull request.
3. Separate Jira Stories are created before GAD-7 runtime, executable support routing, specialist sharing or automatic reminders are scheduled. MB-205 is the tracked progress API/UI Story. No issue key is fabricated for remaining work in this document.
4. MB-181 records the final integrated runtime evidence for features that are actually delivered in Sprint 2.

## Closure assessment

No known Critical or High Review 1 ambiguity is silently treated as resolved. Product questions are approved and versioned; mentor validation is pending; production governance and non-existent runtime are explicitly blocked or deferred.
