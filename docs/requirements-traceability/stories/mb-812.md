MB-812 relates to MB-788's live staging QA. It does not enable production,
rewrite historical financial facts, deploy or execute real transfers.

An explicit default-off staging gate permits only new `sandbox-full-price-v1`
earnings with exact succeeded PAYOS payment and processed successful verified
receipt lineage. Each earning snapshots `staging-demo-paid-v1` and the funding
payment FK. Baseline/legacy rows retain `specialist-earning-v1` and null funding.
FAKE, fixture, unverified and mismatched sources remain non-redeemable. Prepare
and explicit retry revalidate the gate and exact lineage.

Allocation is floor(collected whole VND / frozen purchased-plan credit count),
then specialist amount is floor(allocation * 7000 / 10000). Both stages round
down and leave remainders undistributed. PLUS 5,000/four yields 875/session;
three sessions yield 2,625. PREMIUM 10,000/ten yields 700/session; three yield
2,100. Nominal period allocation and all production/default 300000/210000
economics, 100000 minimum and P7D hold are preserved.

The configured staging minimum/hold are 2000/PT10M. The offline pinned SDK
does not establish PayOS's minimum: 2,000 is the owner-directed fallback and
MB-788 must confirm acceptance before live money movement. One/two sessions
do not reach that fallback, so QA requires three; no catalogue/share change
is authorized. MB-787 daily cap, destination confirmation, idempotency, polling,
reservation and close/retry remain unchanged.

Append-only migration `20261104100000-staging-demo-payout.sql` adds the paired
policy/provenance and positive database payout floor. The service enforces its
configured minimum. Deploy migration before new writers and drain older
earning/payout writers before activation. Canonical logical model and dictionary
are paired with this persistence change.

The contract permits dynamic minimum/hold and adds optional exact
`settlementHoldSeconds`, preserving integer whole `settlementHoldDays` for old
consumers. Web follow-up is required before screen-based MB-788 QA: sync
contracts, remove 100000/7 validator constants and support a sub-day hold
display. No mobile earnings/payout surface is affected. This backend slice
leaves frontend work named rather than changing another repository.
