# MB-760 traceability

[Requirements traceability index](../../requirements-traceability.md)

[MB-760](https://vunguyenkhoi47.atlassian.net/browse/MB-760), Journal/AI owner
Thảo Nguyên Trần, adds [Vietnamese speech QA tooling](../../../ai-voice-agent/qa/README.md)
after merged MB-759 #235. It follows
[ADR 0046](../../adr/0046-ai-voice-companion-contract.md) and
[Voice policy v1](../../policies/companion-voice-policy-v1.md), with no changed
service contract, database, runtime worker behavior or client UI.

The scorer covers 13 synthetic scripts, weighted Unicode WER/CER, false
activation counts, nullable turn/latency/listening observations, model/device/
noise/source grouping and explicit missing baseline/dialect coverage. Human
transcript and speaker-token input is never emitted in aggregate reports.
Mock data is separated from human and TTS-loopback results; real TTS observations
require an exact voice UUID. Existing worker CI scripts include the QA gates.

The operator approved at most $0.50 free credit, 600 seconds STT and 3,000 TTS
characters for the **whole first campaign**, no Claude/Bedrock or paid usage.
[Execution record and acceptance states](../../story-mb-760-vietnamese-speech-benchmark.md)
and [aggregate mock evidence](../../evidence/mb-760-mock-pilot-summary.json)
record zero real provider calls/spend, zero tested devices, zero verified
consenting speakers and zero measured dialects. The account is operator-reported
Build Free with observability OFF, but actual remaining credit and independent
recording/egress/export are unverified. Live benchmark is **BLOCKED_ALLOWANCE**.

The [runbook checkpoint](../../ai-voice-demo-runbook.md#mb-760-mock-pilot-checkpoint)
preserves this gate. Models/voice/denoise are recommendations for later comparison,
not a measured winner. Production Voice stays disabled; MB-762/764 device testing
and future live allowance/privacy/participant evidence remain required.
