# MB-759 traceability

[Requirements traceability index](../../requirements-traceability.md)

[MB-759](https://vunguyenkhoi47.atlassian.net/browse/MB-759), Journal/AI owner
Thảo Nguyên Trần, implements the separate
[Node LiveKit worker](../../../ai-voice-agent/README.md) after merged MB-758 #234.
It consumes the active
[Journal REST contract](../../../contracts/openapi/journal-ai-service-v1.yaml)
and frozen [MB-755 proposal](../../../contracts/proposals/companion-voice-v1.openapi.json)
under [ADR 0046](../../adr/0046-ai-voice-companion-contract.md) and
[Voice policy v1](../../policies/companion-voice-policy-v1.md).

Explicit named AI dispatch and a current Journal lease precede audio. Final
Vietnamese STT is sent only to Journal; only its approved reply reaches TTS.
First audio publication, barge-in, mute, stale/revoked lease, bounded reconnect,
provider failure and shutdown are tested without Cloud. The SDK has no LLM,
recording, transcription output, chat history or content logger. Worker RAM
contains only the current call; no database or persisted domain model changes.
Journal remains global quota/consent/settlement/teardown authority.

[Acceptance and execution evidence](../../story-mb-759-voice-agent.md) maps
21 synthetic HTTP/media tests to the lifecycle and privacy rules.
[Operator checkpoint](../../ai-voice-demo-runbook.md#mb-759-worker-checkpoint)
keeps both worker and Journal Voice disabled until account/privacy, MB-760
speech benchmark and authorized opt-in QA gates pass. Existing accounts suffice;
no live spoken reply, Cloud allowance or acoustic-quality claim is made.
Consultation video, AI Text, owner services and frontend behavior are unchanged.
